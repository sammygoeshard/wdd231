Rosa’s Bakery — Website Site Plan

Rosa’s Bakery is a locally focused bakery offering artisanal pastries, baked goods, and light lunch options in a warm, welcoming community atmosphere. The bakery emphasizes quality ingredients, sustainable sourcing, and personalized service.

Design System

Color Palette

Coffee Brown
#6B4E3D

Used for headers, navigation, buttons, and visual accents.
Cream
#F5EFE6

Used for page backgrounds and content areas for warmth and readability.

Typography

The website uses the Lora font family exclusively to convey a handcrafted, inviting, and professional bakery brand.

  • H1: Primary page titles
  • H2: Major content sections
  • Body Text: Informational and promotional content

Brand Tone

Rosa’s Bakery communicates a warm, friendly, artisanal, and professional tone, reflecting its role as a trusted neighborhood bakery.

Site Structure

Page 1 — Home

Purpose: Welcome visitors and introduce Rosa’s Bakery.

  • Hero introduction section
  • Featured pastries and baked goods
  • Community‑focused messaging
  • Primary Call‑to‑Action: Visit the bakery or explore offerings

Page 2 — Discover

Purpose: Share the bakery’s story and values.

  • Our Story and history
  • Commitment to quality and sustainability
  • Visual gallery of products and bakery atmosphere

Page 3 — Special Orders

Purpose: Allow customers to request custom orders.

  • Special order instructions
  • Inquiry form for custom pastries or bulk orders
  • Clear contact and response expectations

Functional Elements

Navigation

A persistent navigation menu appears on all pages. The current page is visually highlighted. A hamburger menu is used on mobile screens, while a full navigation bar is shown on desktop.

Special Orders Form

  • Customer name
  • Email and phone number
  • Order or request details
  • Customization preferences

Upon submission, a confirmation message is displayed without reloading the page.

Layout & Visual Hierarchy

Pages use a clear visual hierarchy: prominent headings, supporting content, and concise calls‑to‑action. Layouts are responsive and optimized for readability.
